Background

I am a Civil Engineering graduate (B.Tech, 2020) who made a deliberate turn toward research, strategy, and the broader questions of how organisations and societies operate. That pivot grew from a persistent conviction that the most interesting problems sit at the intersection of data and judgment, not within technical silos alone.

My career has centred on research, competitive intelligence, and consulting support. Across roles at Universal Data Solutions supporting McKinsey and Company, and at Jasper Colin Research, I built a track record of delivering structured, high-quality research under tight deadlines in fast-moving consulting environments. I have worked on M&A research, due diligence, market entry strategy, competitive benchmarking, and primary research design, and have managed concurrent workstreams across global client mandates.

I completed a Robotics and Automation workshop at IIT BHU, Varanasi, an experience that reinforced the value of first-principles thinking and precision, both standards I carry into every research engagement.

What Drives Me

Three questions keep returning: How are institutions designed? Where do they break down? What would make them better? They surface in my professional work, in the essays I write, and in the way I read.

Years of deep engagement with India's polity, history, economy, and society, driven initially by civil services preparation, have given me a lens that goes beyond data. I understand the constitutional architecture, the administrative machinery, and the social tensions that shape policy outcomes. That context informs everything I research and write.

My aspiration is management and strategy consulting. I want to work on the kinds of problems where analytical rigour, clear communication, and genuine intellectual engagement all matter at once.
