About me

How I ended up in M&A.

The longer version, from a power plant site to sell-side deals.

01
Background

I graduated in civil engineering in 2020 and started out at NTPC’s Rihand plant in Uttar Pradesh, working on its capacity expansion. Not long after, I made a deliberate move into research.

At Jasper Colin I ran phone and online surveys and desk research for multinational clients. Then I spent two years at Universal Data Solutions on McKinsey engagements, building company profiles, industry reports and financial analyses. I was promoted within a year and named Emerging Star of the Team twice in 2024.

Since May 2026 I’ve been an M&A analyst at MP Advisory, a US boutique firm, working remotely from New Delhi on sell-side deals.

In my last two roles I’ve had three to five projects running at once, and I’ve spent a fair share of my time helping newer analysts find their feet: where to look, how to check their own numbers, and how to tell a clear story with what they find.

What drives me

I like understanding how things actually work, whether it’s a business someone wants to sell or a government scheme that isn’t reaching the people it was meant for. M&A gives me that every day, with real companies and real stakes.

I prepared for the UPSC civil services exam, and that time gave me a deep grounding in Indian polity, geography and public policy, and in how governments and international organisations work. It’s a big reason I can pick up a new sector or a policy question quickly, and it still shapes what I read and write.

Next, I’d like to lead a research team, keep training analysts, and stay close to the decisions the work feeds into.
