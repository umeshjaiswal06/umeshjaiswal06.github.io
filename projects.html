Sell-side M&A Execution at MP Advisory

Five live sell-side mandates, from under $1M to $25M+, worked end to end. For each deal I research the business, build a buyer universe of 800–1,200 strategic, hybrid and financial buyers, and run the outreach that gets 45–50+ of them to sign NDAs. I’ve prepared 15+ sales decks and 3 teasers, contributed to 3 CIMs, and support the financial analysis and buyer meetings that follow. Every step is logged in MadeMarket so senior advisors can see where each process stands. Client names stay confidential.

Sell-side M&A Buyer universes NDAs Teasers and CIMs Sales decks MadeMarket

Financial Analysis at MP Advisory

I build as-reported databooks from QuickBooks TB/GL exports: account mapping, monthly P&L and balance sheet, and reconciliation checks. On top of that come income statement reports, AR/AP aging summaries, revenue by customer, and proof of cash from bank and card statements, all feeding the quality of earnings work on each deal.

M&A Research and Company Profiling for McKinsey Engagements

Over two years, 70+ company profiles, industry reports and financial analyses for McKinsey’s M&A, growth strategy and market entry work, mostly in ICT/TMT and diversified industries. The work ran from target identification to due diligence research, built on S&P Capital IQ and Factiva and delivered in Excel and PowerPoint, usually on three to five engagements at once. I also mentored junior analysts and set up the QC checklists the team used. Named Emerging Star of the Team in Q1 and Q4 2024.

M&A research Due diligence Company profiling S&P Capital IQ Factiva

Research Report: AI and the Employment Hierarchy in India (April 2026)

My own report on how AI is reshaping work in India, from entry-level roles up to the C-suite. It maps India’s AI ecosystem, looks at the effects sector by sector, and ends with recommendations for government, employers and HR leaders. It draws on NASSCOM, Deloitte, WEF, RBI, the Stanford AI Index and government policy documents.
