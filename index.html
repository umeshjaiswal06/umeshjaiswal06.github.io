About me

From civil engineering to M&A.

I studied civil engineering and began my career on site at NTPC’s Rihand power plant. Research drew me in after that, first at Jasper Colin and then for two years on McKinsey engagements.

Since May 2026 I’ve been an M&A analyst at MP Advisory, a US boutique firm. Along the way I’ve mentored and trained newer analysts on how to research well and check their own work.
